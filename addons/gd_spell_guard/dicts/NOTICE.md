# Bundled dictionary notice

GDSpellGuard bundles British and American English Hunspell dictionaries in their
standard `.aff` and `.dic` form.

- `en_GB` identifies itself as the British English dictionary maintained by
  Marco A.G. Pinto and released under the LGPL.
- `en_US` is based on Kevin Atkinson's Pspell/Aspell word list under the LGPL.
  Its affix file is derived from Geoff Kuenning's Ispell affix file under the
  BSD license.

The dictionaries are distributed separately from GDSpellGuard's MIT-licensed source
code. Refer to the upstream dictionary notices and licenses before replacing or
redistributing them:

- https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries/tree/master/en
- https://github.com/marcoagpinto/aoo-mozilla-en-dict

External dictionaries installed by a project remain subject to their own
licenses. Keep their license and README files with the project.
